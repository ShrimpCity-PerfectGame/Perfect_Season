Every figure here was replayed out of the shipped data rather than written down: BOARDS holds 160 team-and-era boards over 1,639 men, Fitzpatrick is on 9 of them across 7 teams (the next most is Frank Gore at 8), and he is the only man at 9. The nine rows are those boards, printed the way the app prints a board title – TEAMS[team][0] and WINDOWS[w]. 821 is how many men appear on more than one board, the same number VERSUS.md uses. No fantasy points appear on screen, deliberately: a board shows a real season with its stats and never its points, so the PPR that made this list worth building is the one thing the film may not show. ?guides=1 shows TikTok’s safe area; ?render=1 fills the viewport.

It is cut to the music, so the two ship together. The cue is the owner’s own (tools/film/gridspin-20-0.m4a), 109.3 bpm, drop at 11.26s, onsets measured on a 0.5495s beat grid to within one frame. Every time in the script is BEAT or BAR arithmetic: the scene changes are downbeats, the nine boards cascade on eighth notes, and the lime count under the name lands on the downbeat the drop lands on. Rendering this silent and dropping any other track over it puts every cut off the beat. Render, then mux – never re-render 900 frames just to attach sound:

node tools/film/render.mjs --film tools/film/tiktok-journeyman.html --out build/film/silent-journeyman.mp4
ffmpeg -y -ss 9.064 -i tools/film/gridspin-20-0.m4a -i build/film/silent-journeyman.mp4 \
  -map 1:v -map 0:a -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 256k -t 15 \
  -af "afade=t=in:st=0:d=0.04,afade=t=out:st=14.6:d=0.4" \
  -movflags +faststart build/film/gridspin-tiktok-journeyman-15s.mp4

No loudnorm, deliberately. That window measures −14.6 LUFS integrated at −1.7 dBTP – already inside half a dB of what the platforms normalise to, and with an LRA of 0.5 there is nothing to even out. loudnorm’s dynamic mode would compress a master that needs none for 0.6 LU nobody can hear. The two fades are real, though: the window is cut out of the middle of a track, so the in-fade kills the click at the splice and the out-fade keeps it from stopping dead under the close card.
